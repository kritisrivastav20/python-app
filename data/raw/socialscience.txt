# SOCIAL SCIENCE – CLASS 5 LEARNING MATERIAL

## 1. Understanding Our World

Social Science helps us understand people, places, communities, history, and the world around us. It teaches us how people live together, how societies developed, and how humans interact with their environment.

Social Science can be divided into several areas. Geography helps us understand Earth, landforms, climate, natural resources, countries, and places. History tells us about people and events of the past. Civics teaches us about communities, governments, rights, duties, and responsibilities.

Learning Social Science helps us understand that people around the world may have different languages, food, clothes, traditions, and ways of life. At the same time, people share many common needs such as food, water, shelter, education, safety, and friendship.

Studying society also helps us become responsible citizens. We learn why rules are necessary, why natural resources should be protected, and why people should respect one another.
